# Data and dependencies

Universe source: [S&P 500 companies financials](https://github.com/datasets/s-and-p-500-companies-financials), datasets / Open Knowledge Foundation; publisher's dataset license ODC-PDDL-1.0. Upstream: Yahoo Finance. See `data/provenance.json` for fetched-file SHA256 and caveat. This project does not claim an upstream commercial market-data license or a verified quote date.

Annual numeric facts: Adobe FY2025 10-K, PayPal FY2025 10-K, NIKE FY2026 10-K; direct sources in `data/annual-facts.json`. Only selected factual figures and independently written research are included, not complete copyrighted reports.

Lucide icons: ISC license. Framework and library license notices remain in their respective npm packages. No company logo artwork is included; letter marks are generic interface labels.

Uploaded case and master prompt were used to derive requirements; their full contents are not published in this repository.
